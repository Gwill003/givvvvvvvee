Frequently asked questions
Are you operating in North Gaza?

Yes, HDF is actively operating in all parts of Gaza, including the North, Central, and South regions, in partnership with local organizations. We post daily updates on our relief efforts. Follow us on Instagram @humandevelopmentfund to see our impact in real-time.

What type of aid does HDF provide in Gaza?

HDF provides essential aid such as food, clean water, medical supplies, sanitation, and shelter to families affected by the crisis. We also work on long-term projects aimed at rebuilding infrastructure and supporting sustainable livelihoods.

Can I see HDF’s work in Gaza in real-time?

Absolutely! We post daily updates, photos, and videos of our ongoing relief efforts in Gaza. Follow us on Instagram @humandevelopmentfund to stay informed and see your contributions at work.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, HDF is a registered nonprofit, and all donations are tax-deductible. After donating, you will receive a receipt for your records.
